How FocusShield Works

Get started in less than two minutes with five simple steps.

1

Download & Extract Package

Download the FocusShield-Extension.zip package from the top navigation and extract the contents onto your computer.

2

Load Unpacked Package in Chrome

Navigate to chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode in the top-right corner, click Load unpacked, and select the extracted extension folder.

3

Sign In to Extension

Click the FocusShield icon in your browser toolbar and sign in with your account to link your browser to your live Firebase database.

4

Configure Focus Schedule & Sites

Use the FocusShield Web Dashboard to set your active focus hours (e.g. 08:00 – 17:00) and add restricted social media domains.

5

Stay Productive & Track Progress

Enjoy automated real-time site interception during focus sessions while tracking saved hours and focus score trends on your dashboard.
